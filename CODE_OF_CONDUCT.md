# Cove Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Cove is built by people with different backgrounds, experiences, interests, and
levels of technical confidence. We want every project space to be welcoming,
respectful, and useful for the people who spend time here.

Everyone participating in Cove is expected to help maintain a community where
people can ask questions, share ideas, disagree, learn, and contribute without
harassment or intimidation.

## Our Standards

Behavior that helps Cove stay healthy includes:

- Being kind, patient, and clear with other people.
- Respecting different viewpoints, experiences, and skill levels.
- Giving feedback that is specific, constructive, and focused on the work.
- Accepting feedback with curiosity and good faith.

Behavior that is not acceptable includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Insults, slurs, demeaning comments, or discriminatory language.
- Unwanted sexual attention, sexualized comments.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately derailing conversations.
- Publishing or sharing someone else's private information without permission.
- Contacting someone repeatedly after they have asked you to stop.
- Abusing project processes, moderation channels, or payment workflows.
- Any other conduct that maintainers reasonably determine is harmful to the
  project or its community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Cove project spaces, including GitHub issues,
pull requests, discussions, reviews, documentation, community chat, extension
repositories, events, and any other official or project-adjacent space.


## Reporting

If you experience or witness conduct that may violate this Code of Conduct,
please report it privately to a Cove maintainer. You can do this through the
Cove Discord or by contacting a maintainer through GitHub. Please do not open a
public issue for sensitive reports.

Reports should include as much detail as you feel comfortable sharing, such as:

- What happened.
- Where and when it happened.
- Who was involved or witnessed it.
- Any links, screenshots, or other context that may help maintainers understand
  the situation.

Maintainers will handle reports with care and respect for the privacy and safety
of everyone involved. Information will be shared only with people who need it to
review and respond to the report.
