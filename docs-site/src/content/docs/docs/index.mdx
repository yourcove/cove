Cove’s documentation is organized by what you need:

- **Tutorial** leads you through a complete first experience, whether you are using Cove or extending it.
- **Guide** helps you accomplish a specific task or understand how part of Cove fits together.
- **Reference** describes records, fields, contracts, permissions, and terminology for precise lookup.

## Choose your path

- **Learn by doing:** Choose a path in the [Tutorial](./tutorial/) to scan your first library or build your first extension.
- **Complete a task:** Open the [Guide](./guides/) for installation, library management, metadata, access, operations, and extension development.
- **Look something up:** Open the [Reference](./reference/) for exact product behavior, starting with [Videos](./reference/videos/) and [Terminology](./terminology/).
