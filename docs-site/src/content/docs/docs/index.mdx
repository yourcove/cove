Cove's documentation is organized around who you are and what you need next.

## Choose your path

- **New to Cove:** Follow [Get Started](./get-started/) to choose a supported installation, complete first-run setup, scan media, and learn the library essentials.
- **Using or administering Cove:** Open the [User Guide](./user/) for browsing, organization, search, metadata, access, backups, and troubleshooting.
- **Building Cove or an extension:** Open [Developer](./developer/) for local development, extension tutorials, architecture, packaging, and APIs.
- **Looking up exact behavior:** Open [Reference](./reference/) for records, fields, contracts, permissions, and [terminology](./terminology/).
